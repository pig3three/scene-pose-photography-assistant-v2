# Pose planning

Vary at least four dimensions: placement, action, body angle, gaze, crop, and camera position. No more than two poses may share the same action/body-angle/gaze/crop combination.

## Body mechanics

- Establish feet and center of gravity before arms and gaze.
- Keep standing knees soft and let shoulders respond naturally to weight shift.
- Give each hand a reason: garment, pocket, hair, prop, anchor, or relaxed swing.
- Use movement cues to prevent frozen expressions.

## Scene interaction hierarchy

1. Walk through available space.
2. Pause near an anchor.
3. Touch or lean only when the anchor is visible and safe.
4. Sit only on a stable, permitted surface.
5. Replace impossible interactions with gaze, garment, or movement actions.

Audit that every pose maps to a visible zone, silhouettes differ, crops form a usable gallery, directions match the subject, and each pose can be called in under 15 seconds.
