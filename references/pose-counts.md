# Pose counts

## Eight-pose sequence

Cover environmental establishment, relaxed static pose, natural motion, real scene interaction, seated or supported substitute, medium emotional portrait, close/profile/over-shoulder moment, and candid finish.

## Sixteen-pose sequence

Create four chapters of four poses:

- A / P01–P04: environmental entry and orientation
- B / P05–P08: movement and safe scene interaction
- C / P09–P12: medium portraits, garment-aware gestures, and gaze changes
- D / P13–P16: close moments, reverse angles, transitions, and candid finish

Vary at least four dimensions across the set: placement, action, body angle, gaze, crop, and camera position. No more than two poses may share the same action/body-angle/gaze/crop combination. Do not pad 16 poses with minor hand or gaze changes.
