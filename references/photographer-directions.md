# Photographer directions

Write spoken Mandarin by default for Chinese users. Use two or three beats: movement or placement, one simple body/gaze adjustment, then a capture cue.

Example:

```text
“沿着路边慢慢往前走，右手轻碰一下外套。看到前面的树时停半秒，不用看我。”
```

Use positive action verbs. Direct one change at a time. Give children a game or destination. Give older adults time to settle and confirm comfort. Use continuous shooting for transitions. Avoid anatomical overload, vague “自然一点”, body judgments, painful imbalance, and sexualized directions for minors.
