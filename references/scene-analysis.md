# Scene analysis

Inspect ground, horizon, perspective, access, obstacles, depth zones, real anchors, light direction, clean backgrounds, and negative space. Select one primary shooting route and at most one fallback.

## Placement rules

- Separate the silhouette from the background.
- Prevent poles, branches, signs, and horizons from cutting through the head or joints.
- Prefer open shade or controlled backlight when direct sun is harsh.
- Use only visible, safe interaction anchors.
- Treat unverified edges, wet rocks, roads, water, construction, fragile furniture, and steep stairs as unsafe.
