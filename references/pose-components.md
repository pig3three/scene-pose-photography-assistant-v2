# Hand and leg pose components

Use this reference for every 8- or 16-pose plan. Select combinations based on subject, mood, wardrobe, scene, crop, and safety; do not mechanically cycle through the library.

## Gesture styles

- `自然`: relaxed open hand, garment touch, hair adjustment, sleeve or cuff adjustment, hand behind back, gentle environmental touch, walking swing.
- `俏皮`: single- or double-hand V, finger heart, two-hand heart, open-palmed wave, playful point, hands framing cheeks, small celebratory lift.
- `克制`: lightly joined hands, one hand holding the opposite wrist, hands behind back, one hand at collar or cuff, restrained pocket gesture when a pocket visibly exists.
- `互动`: reading or pointing toward a real sign, touching a safe wall or rail lightly, holding an existing prop, reacting to wind or fabric.

Do not use a pocket gesture when the locked garment has no visible pocket. Do not invent handheld props. Keep V signs, hearts, cheek framing, or waving age-appropriate and compatible with the requested tone.

## Hand configurations

Choose from distinct silhouettes:

1. both hands released with asymmetric finger spacing
2. one open palm facing slightly outward
3. single-hand V near shoulder, not covering the face
4. double-hand V at unequal heights
5. small finger heart near shoulder or chest
6. two-hand heart at chest level
7. one hand adjusts hair or tucks hair behind ear
8. one hand adjusts cuff, sleeve, collar, or garment edge
9. one hand lightly holds opposite wrist
10. both hands loosely joined low in front
11. both hands behind the back
12. one hand gestures toward a real scene anchor
13. hands open slightly away from the torso during a turn
14. natural opposing arm swing during walking

Keep fingers anatomically plausible and visible. Avoid pressing hands flat against the torso, hiding both hands without purpose, covering facial features, or micro-directing every finger.

## Leg and weight configurations

1. feet parallel with one knee soft
2. front-back staggered stance
3. one leg crosses lightly in front of the other
4. wider stance with weight centered
5. side step with weight moving laterally
6. one knee bent inward or outward while the supporting leg stays stable
7. heel lifted with toe touching the ground
8. toe tap to the side
9. walking mid-stride
10. back foot lifted slightly in a playful finish
11. safe seated diagonal with knees together or staggered, only when a real seat exists
12. half-turn transition with feet forming a stable pivot

Match leg shape to clothing and footwear. Restrict stride in narrow skirts, avoid unstable cross-steps in heels or on uneven ground, and never ask for a bent or lifted leg that creates a fall risk.

## Diversity minimums

For 8 poses:

- use at least 5 clearly different hand configurations
- use at least 5 clearly different leg or weight configurations
- include at least 2 poses with intentionally expressive gestures when the selected style is `俏皮` or `混合`
- do not repeat any exact hand-plus-leg combination
- do not use any single hand configuration or leg configuration more than twice

For 16 poses:

- use at least 8 different hand configurations
- use at least 8 different leg or weight configurations
- distribute expressive gestures across chapters rather than clustering them in one board

## Pose matrix audit

Before output, build an internal matrix with columns: pose ID, hand configuration, leg configuration, weight-bearing leg, body angle, gaze, crop, and movement. Revise duplicates before writing text or generating boards. The user-facing output should describe results, not expose hidden reasoning.
